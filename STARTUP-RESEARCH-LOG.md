### Confirmed evidence (3-0 unless noted)

- **accessiBe / FTC:** $1M order (2025-01-03); accessWidget "did not make all user websites WCAG-compliant… false,
  misleading, or unsubstantiated"; order bars future automated-compliance claims without substantiation. → overlay
  category legally constrained.
- **EU e-invoicing dates:** Belgium 2026-01-01 (Peppol BIS 3.0, no size exemption); Poland KSeF 2026-02 (>PLN 200M) →
  2026-04 (SMEs) → 2027-01 (micro); France 2026-09-01 (receive; large/medium issue) → 2027-09 (small issue).
- **Public records:** RestaurantData 1.1M US/CA records since 1998, built from permits + liquor licenses + business
  registrations (4–24 wk pre-open window); LeadX ingests raw UCC, "decentralized UCC data was a major pain point"; Apify
  liquor-license commodity pricing $0.05/lead + ≥5 competing scrapers (~$4/1,000 records).
- **US regulatory:** click-to-cancel vacated (8th Cir., July 2025); FTC restarted at ANPRM stage (2026-01-30 → comments
  2026-04-13).
- **Verticals busted:** Jackrabbit Dance 4.6/745 @ $49–245/mo; ServiceCore purpose-built for septic/portable sanitation.

### Refuted

- 🔴 "Three 2026 mandates need three separate integrations" (0-3) — Peppol convergence undercuts it.
- 🔴 LeadX efficacy testimonial (KLC Financial / $100M deal) (1-2) — no pricing/customer/revenue disclosed; weak.
